Trauma and Stress Related Disorders

"To live in the body of a survivor is to never be able to leave the scene of the crime. I cannot ignore the fact that I live here."

Trauma and stress related disorders involve mental health conditions that develop after exposure to a traumatic or highly stressful event. These disorders affect emotional, psychological, and physical well-being, and the symptoms often disrupt a person's ability to function normally in everyday life.

Types of Trauma and Stress Related Disorders

Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD)

PTSD occurs after experiencing or witnessing a life-threatening event.

  • Intrusive thoughts and flashbacks
  • Emotional numbness
  • Hypervigilance
  • Avoidance behaviors

Acute Stress Disorder (ASD)

A short-term condition occurring immediately after trauma.

  • Dissociation
  • Intrusive memories
  • Emotional distress
  • Heightened arousal

Common Symptoms

Emotional Symptoms

  • Anxiety and fear
  • Depression
  • Emotional numbness
  • Guilt or shame

Physical Symptoms

  • Sleep disturbances
  • Headaches
  • Fatigue
  • Muscle tension

Behavioral Symptoms

  • Avoidance
  • Hypervigilance
  • Irritability
  • Social withdrawal

Treatment Options

Psychotherapy

  • Cognitive Behavioral Therapy
  • EMDR
  • Exposure Therapy
  • Group Therapy

Medication

  • Antidepressants
  • Anti-anxiety medication
  • Sleep aids
  • Mood stabilizers

Support Systems

  • Support groups
  • Family therapy
  • Peer counseling
  • Community resources

Self-Care

  • Mindfulness practices
  • Regular exercise
  • Stress management
  • Healthy lifestyle
