Understanding Mental Health

Mental health plays a vital role in shaping our overall well-being, influencing how we think, feel, and behave daily. It encompasses our emotional, psychological, and social state and determines how we handle stress, relate to others, and make choices in life.

The Importance of Mental Health

Good mental health is much more than the absence of mental illness. It is the foundation for thriving in our personal lives, careers, and relationships. It enables us to cope with life's challenges, recover from setbacks, adapt to change, and engage meaningfully with others.

Types of Mental Health Disorders

Anxiety Disorders

Anxiety disorders involve excessive fear or worry that interferes with daily life.

Mood Disorders

Mood disorders primarily affect a person's emotional state.

Obsessive-Compulsive Disorders

These disorders involve intrusive thoughts and repetitive behaviors.

Trauma-Related Disorders

These disorders develop after exposure to traumatic events.
