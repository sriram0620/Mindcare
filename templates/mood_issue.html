Mood Disorders

"Sometimes it takes an overwhelming breakdown to have an undeniable breakthrough"

What are Mood Disorders?

A mood disorder is a mental health condition that primarily affects your emotional state. It's a disorder in which you experience long periods of extreme happiness, extreme sadness, or both. Mood disorders can cause changes in your behavior and affect your ability to perform routine activities.

Types of Mood Disorders

Depression

Feeling sad or hopeless for long periods, losing interest in activities once enjoyed, and difficulty thinking, memory, eating, and sleeping.

Bipolar Disorder

Alternating periods of emotional highs (mania or hypomania) and lows (depression).

Symptoms of Mood Disorders

Depressive Symptoms

  • Feeling sad or hopeless most of the day
  • Loss of interest in activities
  • Significant weight changes
  • Sleep disturbances

Manic Symptoms

  • Feeling overly happy for long periods
  • Decreased need for sleep
  • Racing thoughts and fast speech
  • Impulsive behaviors

Treatment Options

Medications

  • Antidepressants
  • Mood stabilizers
  • Antipsychotics

Psychotherapy

  • Cognitive behavioral therapy
  • Dialectical behavior therapy
  • Group therapy

Other Treatments

  • Electroconvulsive therapy
  • Transcranial magnetic stimulation
  • Light therapy
